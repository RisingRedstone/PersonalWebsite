About Me

I'm a Master of IT (AI Specialization) student at UNSW Sydney with a deep passion for building systems that push the boundaries of what's possible — from billion-ops-per-second concurrent data structures to multi-agent reinforcement learning environments.

My work spans the full spectrum: I write CUDA kernels for GPU-accelerated rendering, design lock-free concurrency primitives in C++23, implement neural networks from scratch in PyTorch, and architect production-grade async data pipelines. I believe the most interesting engineering happens at the intersection of AI and systems.

Previously, I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science at SRM Institute (CGPA 8.88/10), where I published research on vision-based danger assessment at ICVGIP '24 and won multiple hackathons. I've built everything from matching engines benchmarked at 650M ops/sec to edge-optimized recursive-thinking LLMs.
